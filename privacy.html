PharmaCore — Privacy Policy

PharmaCore is a personal study app for memorizing drug names and clinical facts. It has no company, no server, and no account system of its own.

What stays on your device

All of your drug library data (chapters, drugs, notes, tables) is stored locally in your browser's storage on your own device. It never passes through any server operated by the app's author.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app stores a single JSON file containing your drug library in your own Google Drive, inside the Drive application data folder (drive.appdata scope) — a hidden, private space tied only to this app. This file:

  • is never visible in your normal Drive file list,
  • is never shared with anyone else,
  • is never read by the app's author or any third party,
  • is used only to let your own devices, signed in with your own Google account, stay in sync with each other.

The app also requests your Google account email address (userinfo.email scope) solely to show you which account is currently connected inside the app's Cloud Sync screen.

What the app does not do

PharmaCore does not run analytics, does not use advertising, does not sell or share data with any third party, and does not access any other files in your Google Drive beyond the single app-specific sync file described above.

AI features (optional, bring-your-own-key)

If you use the optional AI features (prescription scanning, clinical scenarios, quiz generation), you supply your own API key for your chosen provider (Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini). That key is stored only in your device's local browser storage and is sent only directly to that provider when you use those features — never to any server operated by this app's author.

Contact

This is an independent personal project. Questions can be directed to the app's developer via the contact details on its GitHub repository.
